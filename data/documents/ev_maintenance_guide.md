# Electric Vehicle (EV) Ownership and Maintenance Guide

## EV Maintenance vs Internal Combustion Engines (ICE)
Electric vehicles have approximately 20 moving parts in their drivetrain compared to over 2,000 moving parts in petrol/diesel engines.

### Key Maintenance Differences:
1. **No Engine Oil or Oil Filters**: Eliminates recurring oil change costs every 10,000 km.
2. **No Spark Plugs, Fuel Injectors, Timing Belts, or Radiator Flush**.
3. **Brake Pad Longevity**: Due to regenerative braking (where the electric motor slows the car while generating power), friction brake pads routinely last over 80,000 to 100,000 km.
4. **Tire Wear & Rotation**: EVs produce instant torque from zero RPM, which can wear tires slightly faster. Regular rotation every 10,000 km and maintaining correct tire pressure is essential.
5. **Battery Thermal Management**: Modern liquid-cooled battery packs (IP67 sealed) require coolant inspection every 30,000 km.

## Battery Life and Warranty
- Indian market EVs typically feature IP67-rated Lithium-Iron-Phosphate (LFP) or Nickel-Manganese-Cobalt (NMC) chemistries designed for 1,500 to 2,000 full charge cycles.
- This equates to over 300,000 km to 450,000 km before battery capacity degrades to 80%.
- OEM warranties typically cover 8 Years or 160,000 km for the battery pack and electric motor.
