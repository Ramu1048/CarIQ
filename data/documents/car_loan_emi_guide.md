# Car Loan Financing, EMI Concepts & Down Payment Guide

## Understanding Car Loan Mathematics
When purchasing a vehicle on finance, several fundamental components determine monthly outgo:

### 1. Principal Loan Amount (P)
The exact sum borrowed from a bank or NBFC after deducting the upfront down payment from the on-road price.
$$\text{Principal} = \text{On-Road Price} - \text{Down Payment}$$

### 2. Down Payment
The initial upfront amount paid by the buyer from their savings. A recommended down payment is 20% to 25% of the total on-road price to keep interest costs reasonable and prevent negative equity.

### 3. Loan Tenure (n)
The total repayment period in months, typically ranging between 36 months (3 years) to 84 months (7 years).
- Shorter tenures (3-5 years) mean higher monthly EMI but substantially lower total interest paid.
- Longer tenures (7 years) lower monthly EMI but significantly increase overall interest.

### 4. Equated Monthly Installment (EMI) Formula
The standard reducing balance formula used by Indian financial institutions:
$$EMI = \frac{P \times r \times (1 + r)^n}{(1 + r)^n - 1}$$
Where:
- $P$ = Principal loan amount
- $r$ = Monthly interest rate ($\frac{\text{Annual Interest Rate}}{12 \times 100}$)
- $n$ = Total tenure in months

### Example EMI Scenarios (at 8.75% Interest Rate):
- **Loan Amount: ₹10,00,000 for 5 Years (60 Months)**:
  - Monthly EMI: ~₹20,637
  - Total Interest Paid: ~₹2,38,209
  - Total Repayment: ~₹12,38,209
- **Loan Amount: ₹15,00,000 for 5 Years (60 Months)**:
  - Monthly EMI: ~₹30,955
  - Total Interest Paid: ~₹3,57,314
  - Total Repayment: ~₹18,57,314

## Additional On-Road Costs Beyond Ex-Showroom
- **RTO / Road Tax**: 8% to 15% depending on state and fuel type (EVs often get 0% to 5% concessional road tax).
- **Comprehensive Insurance**: 1 Year Own Damage + 3 Years Third Party (~₹35,000 to ₹75,000).
- **TCS (Tax Collected at Source)**: 1% for vehicles with ex-showroom price above ₹10 Lakh.
- **Fastag & Registration Handling**: ~₹1,500 - ₹3,000.
