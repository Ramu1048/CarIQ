# Tata Punch EV - Electric Micro SUV Guide

## Overview
The Tata Punch EV is built on Tata's acti.ev pure EV architecture, delivering instant electric torque, 5-star Bharat NCAP crash safety, and ultra-low running costs of approx ₹1.10 per kilometer.

## Specifications
- **Brand**: Tata Motors
- **Model**: Punch EV
- **Vehicle ID**: tata_punch_ev
- **Segment**: Micro SUV EV
- **Price Range**: ₹10.99 Lakh to ₹15.49 Lakh (Ex-Showroom)
- **Battery Pack**:
  - Medium Range: 25 kWh (Claimed 315 km, Real-world 220 km)
  - Long Range: 35 kWh (Claimed 421 km, Real-world 290-310 km)
- **Electric Motor Power**: 120 bhp (90 kW)
- **Instant Torque**: 190 Nm
- **0-100 km/h Acceleration**: 9.5 seconds
- **Charging Time**:
  - 50 kW DC Fast Charger: 10% to 80% in 56 minutes
  - 7.2 kW AC Home Wallbox: 0% to 100% in 5.0 hours
  - 3.3 kW 15A Portable Charger: 0% to 100% in 9.4 hours
- **Boot Space**: 366 Litres + 14 Litres Front Trunk (Frunk)
- **Ground Clearance**: 190 mm
- **Water Wading Depth**: 350 mm (IP67 Ingress Protection for Battery & Motor)

## Safety & Bharat NCAP Rating
- **Bharat NCAP Rating**: 5 Stars (31.46 / 32 points for Adult, 45 / 49 points for Child).
- **Airbags**: 6 Airbags standard on all variants.
- **Safety Technology**: Electronic Stability Program (ESP), Hill Descent Control, Auto-Hold, All 4-wheel Disc Brakes (Long Range), Multi-Mode Regenerative Braking with Steering Paddle Shifters.

## Technology & Comfort
- 10.25-inch High-Definition Touchscreen by HARMAN with Arcade.ev App Suite (YouTube, OTT apps).
- 10.25-inch Digital Cockpit Instrument Display with navigation projection.
- Ventilated Front Seats.
- 360-degree camera with 3D surround view and blind-spot feed.
- V2L (Vehicle to Load) charging capability allowing powering of laptops and camping equipment.

## Maintenance & Operating Economics
- **Running Cost**: ₹1.10 - ₹1.40 / km (Home charging at ₹7.5 / kWh).
- **Annual Maintenance**: ₹3,000 - ₹4,000 (No oil changes, minimal friction brake wear due to regen).
- **Battery & Motor Warranty**: 8 Years or 160,000 km.
- **Vehicle Warranty**: 3 Years or 125,000 km.
