# Tata Nexon - Complete Technical & Buyer's Guide

## Overview
The Tata Nexon is a compact SUV in the B-segment known for its class-leading crash test safety rating, rugged high ground clearance, and futuristic design.

## Specifications
- **Brand**: Tata Motors
- **Model**: Nexon (2024 Facelift)
- **Vehicle ID**: tata_nexon
- **Segment**: Compact SUV
- **Price Range**: ₹8.00 Lakh to ₹15.80 Lakh (Ex-Showroom)
- **Engine**: 1.2L Turbocharged Revotron Petrol (1199 cc, 3-cylinder)
- **Max Power**: 118 bhp @ 5500 RPM
- **Max Torque**: 170 Nm @ 1750-4000 RPM
- **Transmission**: 6-speed Manual / 6-speed AMT / 7-speed Dual-Clutch Automatic (DCA)
- **Fuel Economy**: 17.01 km/l (ARAI Petrol DCA), 23.2 km/l (Diesel MT)
- **Seating Capacity**: 5 Persons
- **Boot Space**: 382 Litres
- **Ground Clearance**: 208 mm
- **Fuel Tank Capacity**: 44 Litres

## Safety & Crash Test Rating
- **Global NCAP Rating**: 5 Stars for Adult Occupant Protection, 5 Stars for Child Occupant Protection.
- **Airbags**: 6 Airbags standard across all variants.
- **Active Safety**: Electronic Stability Program (ESP), Hill Hold Control, Roll-over Mitigation, Traction Control, ISOFIX child seat mounts, Tyre Pressure Monitoring System (TPMS), and 360-Degree Surround Camera with Blind Spot View Monitor.

## Key Features & Technology
- 10.25-inch Floating Touchscreen Infotainment by HARMAN with Wireless Android Auto & Apple CarPlay.
- 10.25-inch Fully Digital Instrument Cluster with Full Map View Navigation.
- Ventilated Front Seats (Driver and Co-driver).
- Voice Assisted Electric Sunroof.
- Air Purifier with AQI display.
- Wireless Smartphone Charger & 45W Type-C fast charging.
- Multi-drive modes: Eco, City, and Sport.

## Maintenance & Ownership Cost
- **Annual Scheduled Maintenance**: ₹8,000 - ₹9,500
- **Warranty**: 3 Years / 100,000 km standard warranty, extendable up to 5 years.
- **Service Intervals**: Every 10,000 km or 12 months.

## Pros & Cons
- **Pros**: 5-star crash safety, best-in-class 208mm ground clearance, plush ride over bad roads, high-tech dual-screen interior.
- **Cons**: 3-cylinder engine thrum under hard acceleration, tight rear legroom for tall adults when front seats are pushed back.
