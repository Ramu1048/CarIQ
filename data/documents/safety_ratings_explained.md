# Car Safety Standards, Global NCAP & Bharat NCAP Explained

## Crash Test Protocols
Safety testing evaluates how well a vehicle protects passengers during collisions:

### 1. Bharat NCAP & Global NCAP Protocols
- **Frontal Impact Offset Crash Test**: Vehicle crashes into a deformable barrier at 64 km/h with 40% overlap.
- **Side Mobile Deformable Barrier Test**: A 1,400 kg barrier strikes the side of the vehicle at 50 km/h.
- **Side Pole Impact Test**: Vehicle is propelled sideways into a rigid pole at 29 km/h to test head and chest curtain airbag protection.
- **Pedestrian Safety**: Evaluates head and leg impact injury risk to pedestrians at 40 km/h.

### 2. Active Safety Features vs Passive Safety
- **Passive Safety**: Protects occupants during and after a crash:
  - High-strength steel safety cage / monocoque structure
  - Airbags (Front, Side, Curtain, Knee)
  - 3-point ELR seatbelts with pre-tensioners & load limiters
  - ISOFIX child seat anchor points
- **Active Safety**: Helps the driver avoid crashes altogether:
  - Electronic Stability Control (ESC/ESP)
  - Anti-lock Braking System (ABS) with Electronic Brakeforce Distribution (EBD)
  - Traction Control System (TCS) & Hill Hold Control
  - Advanced Driver Assistance Systems (ADAS Level 2): Autonomous Emergency Braking (AEB), Lane Keeping Assist (LKA), Adaptive Cruise Control (ACC), Blind Spot Monitoring (BSM).
