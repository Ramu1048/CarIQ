# CarIQ Frequently Asked Questions & Buying Advice

## 1. Petrol vs Diesel vs Strong Hybrid vs EV: Which should I choose?
- **Choose Petrol** if monthly running is under 1,000 km, primarily short city trips, and initial purchase budget is a key constraint.
- **Choose Diesel** if monthly highway running exceeds 1,500 - 2,000 km, you need strong low-end torque for heavy loads or 7-seater vehicles (e.g., XUV700, Harrier), and you don't live in NCR (where 10-year diesel ban applies).
- **Choose Strong Hybrid** (e.g., Grand Vitara, Hyryder, City e:HEV) if you want 25-28 km/l mileage in heavy city traffic without needing to plug into a charger or worry about battery range.
- **Choose Electric (EV)** (e.g., Punch EV, Tiago EV) if you have home charging access and daily city commute of 30-100 km, seeking lowest cost per kilometer (₹1.20/km vs ₹7-9/km for petrol).

## 2. Manual vs Automatic Transmissions Explained
- **AMT (Automated Manual Transmission)**: Lowest cost, great fuel efficiency (e.g., Swift AMT, Tiago AMT), slight pause during aggressive gear changes.
- **Torque Converter (AT)**: Most reliable, robust, smooth gear shifts (e.g., Brezza AT, XUV700 AT).
- **CVT / IVT**: Stepless, ultra-smooth city acceleration with zero shift shock (e.g., Creta IVT).
- **DCT / DSG (Dual Clutch)**: Lightning fast gear shifts, enthusiastic performance (e.g., Nexon DCA, Seltos DCT, Verna Turbo DCT).
- **e-CVT**: Used exclusively in strong hybrids, seamlessly blends electric motor and petrol engine power.

## 3. What is Ground Clearance and why does it matter in India?
- Ground clearance is the minimum distance between the lowest point of the vehicle's undercarriage and the flat road.
- Indian roads and speed bumps generally require:
  - **Sedans/Hatchbacks**: 160mm - 175mm (drive carefully over steep ramps)
  - **Compact & Midsize SUVs**: 190mm - 210mm (effortlessly clears bad potholes, monsoon waterlogging, and high speed breakers).

## 4. How much should I spend on a car?
A proven financial rule of thumb is the **20/4/10 Rule**:
- Make a down payment of at least **20%**.
- Finance the car for no more than **4 years (48 months)**.
- Total vehicle monthly costs (EMI + fuel + insurance + maintenance) should not exceed **10% to 15% of your gross monthly income**.
