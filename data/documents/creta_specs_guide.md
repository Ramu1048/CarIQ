# Hyundai Creta - Technical & Buyer's Guide

## Overview
The Hyundai Creta is the benchmark midsize SUV in India, celebrated for its premium interior aesthetics, seamless IVT transmission, Level 2 ADAS suite, and plush ride quality.

## Specifications
- **Brand**: Hyundai
- **Model**: Creta (2024 Facelift)
- **Vehicle ID**: hyundai_creta
- **Segment**: Midsize SUV
- **Price Range**: ₹11.00 Lakh to ₹20.15 Lakh (Ex-Showroom)
- **Engine Options**:
  - 1.5L MPi Naturally Aspirated Petrol (113 bhp, 144 Nm)
  - 1.5L U2 CRDi Turbo Diesel (114 bhp, 250 Nm)
  - 1.5L Turbo GDi Petrol (158 bhp, 253 Nm)
- **Transmission**: 6-speed MT / IVT (CVT) / 6-speed AT (Diesel) / 7-speed DCT (Turbo Petrol)
- **Fuel Economy**: 17.7 km/l (ARAI Petrol IVT), 21.8 km/l (Diesel MT), 18.4 km/l (Turbo DCT)
- **Seating Capacity**: 5 Persons
- **Boot Space**: 433 Litres
- **Ground Clearance**: 190 mm

## Safety & ADAS Level 2
- **Safety Rating**: 5 Stars
- **Airbags**: 6 Airbags standard across all variants.
- **Hyundai SmartSense Level 2 ADAS**: 19 safety features including Forward Collision-Avoidance Assist, Lane Keeping Assist, Blind-Spot Collision-Avoidance, Smart Cruise Control with Stop & Go, Leading Vehicle Departure Alert, and High Beam Assist.
- **Surround View**: 360-degree camera with Blind-spot View Monitor (BVM) in the digital cluster.

## Key Features & Luxury Amenities
- Panoramic Sunroof with Voice Activation.
- Dual Integrated 10.25-inch Touchscreen and Digital Cockpit.
- 8-Speaker Premium Bose Audio System.
- Ventilated Front Seats & 8-Way Powered Driver Seat.
- Dual-Zone Automatic Climate Control.
- Bluelink connected car tech with 70+ remote features.

## Ownership & Maintenance
- **Annual Maintenance**: ₹8,500 - ₹10,000
- **Warranty**: 3 Years / Unlimited km standard warranty.
