# Maruti Suzuki Brezza - Technical & Buyer's Guide

## Overview
The Maruti Suzuki Brezza is India's highest-selling reliable sub-4m compact SUV powered by the refined 1.5-litre K15C naturally aspirated engine with Smart Hybrid technology.

## Specifications
- **Brand**: Maruti Suzuki
- **Model**: Brezza
- **Vehicle ID**: maruti_brezza
- **Segment**: Compact SUV
- **Price Range**: ₹8.34 Lakh to ₹14.14 Lakh (Ex-Showroom)
- **Engine**: 1.5L K15C DualJet Dual VVT Petrol (1462 cc, 4-cylinder)
- **Max Power**: 102 bhp @ 6000 RPM
- **Max Torque**: 137 Nm @ 4400 RPM
- **Transmission**: 5-speed Manual / 6-speed Torque Converter Automatic with Paddle Shifters
- **Fuel Economy**: 19.80 km/l (ARAI Petrol AT), 17.38 km/l (MT), 25.51 km/kg (CNG)
- **Seating Capacity**: 5 Persons
- **Boot Space**: 328 Litres
- **Ground Clearance**: 200 mm
- **Fuel Tank Capacity**: 48 Litres

## Safety & Structural Integrity
- **Global NCAP Rating**: 4 Stars
- **Airbags**: 6 Airbags on ZXi+ variants, Dual Airbags standard on lower trims.
- **Safety Equipment**: ESP, Hill Hold Assist, ABS with EBD, 360-degree camera, Rear Parking Sensors, ISOFIX mounts.

## Key Features & Technology
- Colored Head-Up Display (HUD) showing speed, tachometer, fuel economy, and turn navigation.
- 360-Degree High-Definition View Camera.
- 9-inch SmartPlay Pro+ Touchscreen Infotainment with Wireless Apple CarPlay & Android Auto.
- Electric Sunroof.
- Arkamys SoundSense tuned audio.
- Wireless Phone Charging Pad.
- Suzuki Connect telematics with 40+ connected car functions.

## Maintenance & Ownership Cost
- **Annual Maintenance**: ₹5,500 - ₹6,500 (Lowest in compact SUV category).
- **Warranty**: 2 Years / 40,000 km standard warranty, extendable up to 5 Years / 100,000 km.
- **Service Intervals**: Every 10,000 km or 12 months.

## Pros & Cons
- **Pros**: Outstanding fuel efficiency (up to 19.8 km/l), ultra-smooth 4-cylinder engine refinement, lowest cost of ownership, hassle-free nationwide service network.
- **Cons**: Lack of punchy turbo acceleration, boot space of 328L is smaller than Nexon/Creta.
