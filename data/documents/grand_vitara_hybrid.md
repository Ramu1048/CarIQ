# Maruti Suzuki Grand Vitara Strong Hybrid - Guide

## Overview
The Maruti Suzuki Grand Vitara Strong Hybrid features Toyota's self-charging Full Hybrid system (e-CVT), delivering exceptional fuel efficiency of 27.97 km/l without requiring any external plug-in charging.

## Specifications
- **Brand**: Maruti Suzuki
- **Model**: Grand Vitara
- **Vehicle ID**: maruti_grand_vitara_hybrid
- **Segment**: Midsize Hybrid SUV
- **Price Range**: ₹10.80 Lakh to ₹20.09 Lakh (Strong Hybrid starts at ₹18.43 Lakh)
- **Engine**: 1.5L TNGA 3-Cylinder Atkinson Cycle Petrol + Synchronous AC Electric Motor
- **Combined Hybrid Power**: 114 bhp (85 kW)
- **Engine Torque**: 122 Nm + Electric Motor Torque: 141 Nm
- **Transmission**: Electronic Continuously Variable Transmission (e-CVT)
- **ARAI Fuel Efficiency**: 27.97 km/l (One of the most fuel-efficient SUVs in India)
- **Fuel Tank Capacity**: 45 Litres (Theoretical highway cruising range > 1,150 km per tank)
- **Seating Capacity**: 5 Persons
- **Boot Space**: 265 Litres (Reduced slightly due to Lithium-ion battery under floor)
- **Ground Clearance**: 210 mm

## How Strong Hybrid Works
- **EV Mode**: The car drives silently on pure electric battery power during low speeds and stop-and-go city traffic (up to 40-50 km/h).
- **Hybrid Driving**: In acceleration, both petrol engine and electric motor work together seamlessly for punchy response.
- **Regenerative Braking**: Every time the driver slows down or brakes, kinetic energy is converted into electricity and stored in the battery pack.
- **No Charging Plug Required**: The car recharges itself while driving and coasting.
