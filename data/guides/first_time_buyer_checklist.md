# First-Time Car Buyer Checklist & Pre-Delivery Inspection (PDI)

## 1. Budgeting Checklist
- Ex-Showroom Price + State RTO Road Tax + Comprehensive Insurance + Fastag + Registration.
- Remember annual fuel and service expenses (approx ₹60,000 - ₹1,20,000 / year depending on usage).
- Extended warranty and roadside assistance (RSA) are strongly recommended.

## 2. Pre-Delivery Inspection (PDI) Checklist
Before accepting vehicle delivery and signing registration papers:
1. **Odometer Reading**: Ensure reading is under 50-100 km (indicating vehicle was driven only from stockyard).
2. **Body & Paint**: Inspect under natural sunlight for paint swirls, scratches, panel gap mismatches, or repainted panels.
3. **Glass & Lights**: Inspect windshield, windows, headlamps, and tail lights for chips, cracks, or fogging.
4. **Tires**: Check brand and manufacturing week/year (DOT code on tire sidewall). Verify spare tire and toolkit are present.
5. **Electricals**: Test all power windows, sunroof, air conditioning, digital screens, reverse camera, headlights, fog lamps, and horn.
6. **Documentation**: Match VIN (Vehicle Identification Number) and Engine Number on the car with Invoice, Insurance cover note, and Form 20/22.
